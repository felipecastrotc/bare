## Change: Raise test coverage to 80% across src/bare

## Why
- Current coverage is ~38%, leaving critical backup/mount orchestration and finders untested and blocking the newly enforced 80% threshold.
- Higher coverage reduces regressions in CLI flows, mount handling, and external command wrappers that depend on platform-specific behavior.

## What Changes
- Add targeted unit/integration-style tests to raise line/branch coverage to at least 80% for `src/bare`, focusing on orchestration modules (`main`, `mount_manager`, `backup`, `destination_handler`, finder layers, utils).
- Refactor code only as needed to enable testability (dependency injection, command stubs, small seams) without altering behavior.
- Keep coverage gating at 80% and ensure the suite passes under the CI matrix (3.11–3.13).

## Impact
- Affected specs: coverage-baseline
- Affected code: new/updated tests under `tests/`, minor testability seams in `src/bare/*` as required, coverage reports generated by existing Pixi/CI tasks.
